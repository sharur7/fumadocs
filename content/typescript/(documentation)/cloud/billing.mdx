**Billing** holds your organization's payment methods and invoices. Plans are set per app: each app has its own Free or Pro plan, usage, and overage settings, which you change from the app's [Billing tab](/docs/cloud/apps/billing). **View Apps** takes you there.

## Payment methods

Cards saved under **Payment methods** pay for every Pro app's monthly invoice and for top-ups. Click **Add method** to add a card.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/billing.png" alt="The organization Billing page: a note that plan changes live in each app's Billing tab, with View Apps, above the Payment methods tab and an Add method button" />

## Invoices

The **Invoices** tab lists past invoices, including per-app subscriptions and top-ups. Use the filter on the right to narrow the list.

## What this page doesn't bill

Two kinds of charge are managed elsewhere, though they're paid with the cards saved here:

- **App plans.** Upgrading, downgrading, and extra requests are set on each app's [Billing](/docs/cloud/apps/billing) page.
- **Credit top-ups.** Studio tokens and NitroChat credits are topped up on [Studio usage and NitroChat usage](/docs/cloud/usage). Both screens can also top up automatically when the balance falls below an amount you choose.

Because every one of those charges uses a saved card, add your card here first.
