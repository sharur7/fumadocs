Your organization has two credit balances, one for each AI product. The **Studio Tokens** and **NitroChat Credits** cards on the [dashboard](/docs/cloud/dashboard#studio-tokens-and-nitrochat-credits) summarize them; each opens a usage screen where you see the details and add credit.

| Balance | What spends it | Screen |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| **Studio tokens** | Model calls that your team makes in NitroStudio. | Studio usage |
| **NitroChat credits** | Model calls that your hosted chats make. | NitroChat usage |

Top-ups are charged to a card saved on the organization's [Billing](/docs/cloud/billing) page, so add one there first.

## Studio usage

**Studio usage** shows the Studio token balance and what has been spent.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/studio-usage.png" alt="The Studio usage page: token and dollar balances, usage counters, an auto top-up setting, a performance history chart, and a call log" />

- **Top up credits** adds credit now, charged to a saved card.
- **Top up when balance falls below** sets an automatic top-up. When the balance drops under the amount you enter, NitroCloud charges a saved card before the pool runs out, so your team isn't cut off mid-task.

Who can use Studio, and how much each person can spend, is set in [Studio management](/docs/cloud/studio-management).

## NitroChat usage

**NitroChat usage** works the same way for hosted chats: the credit balance, what has been spent, a manual top-up, and an automatic top-up threshold.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/nitrochat-usage.png" alt="The NitroChat usage page: the credit balance and spend, a performance history chart, a breakdown by key, and an auto top-up setting" />

Each chat's own spending is also shown on its [Gateway API key](/docs/cloud/apps/chat#gateway-api-key) card, as credits used against the key's limit.

## Your first top-up

A new organization starts on free token credits. The first paid top-up, on either screen, moves the organization to dollar billing. From then on, the member credit limits and the organization usage limit in [Studio management](/docs/cloud/studio-management#while-you-are-on-free-credits) are enforced in dollars.
