Each app has its own plan, so one organization can run a free prototype next to a paid production app. Open the app and click **Billing**.

Payment methods belong to the organization, not the app. Add a card on the organization's [Billing](/docs/cloud/billing) page before you upgrade.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/app-billing.png" alt="App billing on the Free plan: 500,000 requests a month, current usage, and an empty invoice list" />

## Plans

| Plan | Included | Price |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Free** | 500,000 requests a month. Requests above that are refused with HTTP 429. | No card needed. |
| **Pro** | 5,000,000 requests a month. | $30 a month, plus $0.05 per 10,000 requests beyond the included amount. |

### Upgrade to Pro

Click **Upgrade to Pro** and pick one of the organization's saved cards to pay with. The dialog shows the base fee and the overage rate, a field for a coupon code, and the payment method before you confirm.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/app-billing-upgrade.png" alt="The Upgrade to Pro dialog with the base fee, overage rate, a coupon code field, the payment method, and Cancel and Confirm upgrade buttons" />

### Downgrade to Free

**Downgrade to Free** doesn't take effect immediately. The app stays on Pro until the date shown on the page. On that date the included amount drops to 500,000 requests and extra requests turn off. Overage already used in the period is still billed on the final invoice.

Changed your mind before that date? **Stay on Pro** cancels the scheduled downgrade.

## Usage

**Usage** shows the requests in the current period against the plan's included amount. On Pro, once you pass the included amount with extra requests turned on, the page also shows the overage count and an estimated charge for this cycle.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/app-billing-pro.png" alt="App billing on the Pro plan: the plan with its pricing, period, and Active status, and usage against 5,000,000 included requests" />

## Extra requests

On Pro, **Extra requests** lets the app keep serving after its 5,000,000 included requests instead of refusing them. You set a cap on how many extra requests to allow, up to twice the included amount: 10,000,000 extra, for 15,000,000 in total. Each block of 10,000 extra requests costs $0.05.

Turn extra requests off, and requests above the included amount are refused with HTTP 429 again.

## Invoices

**Invoices** on this page are for this app only. On Pro, **Upcoming invoice** shows the next bill so far: the base fee plus any overage. Invoices for the whole organization, and for credit top-ups, are on the organization's [Billing](/docs/cloud/billing) page.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/app-billing-pro-lower.png" alt="The lower part of Pro app billing: the Extra requests switch, the upcoming invoice with the Pro plan fee, and the list of paid invoices with View and PDF links" />

## When a payment fails

If a charge fails, the app's status changes to **Past due**. During the grace period, the app is limited to the Free plan's quota of 500,000 requests a month, and a banner shows how many days are left.

**Retry payment** charges a saved card again; add or update a card on [Billing](/docs/cloud/billing) first if the old one is the problem. If the invoice is still unpaid when the grace period ends, the app moves to the Free plan, with extra requests off. Overage already used is still on the final invoice.
