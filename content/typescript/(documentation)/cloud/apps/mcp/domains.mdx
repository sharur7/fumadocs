By default, your MCP server is served from a `nitrocloud.ai` address. **Domains**, in the MCP menu, lets you serve it from a hostname you own, such as `mcp.example.com`. NitroCloud verifies the hostname and issues its HTTPS certificate for you.

The chat has its own [Domains](/docs/cloud/apps/chat/domains) page. Adding a domain here doesn't change the chat's URL.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/mcp-domains.png" alt="The MCP Domains page with a field for your domain and an Add domain button" />

## Add a domain

<Steps>
<Step>

### Enter the hostname

Open **Domains** in the MCP menu and enter the hostname, such as `mcp.example.com`, then click **Add domain**.

Use a subdomain. A root domain such as `example.com` only works if your DNS host supports a CNAME-style record at the root.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the CNAME record

The new row shows the target NitroCloud wants your hostname to point at. At your DNS host, create this record:

| Type | Name | Value |
|------|------|-------|
| `CNAME` | The hostname you entered | The target shown on the row |

If the row also shows an `_acme-challenge` CNAME, add that record too. It lets NitroCloud prove you own the hostname so it can issue the certificate.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/mcp-domains-pending-dns.png" alt="A custom domain in Pending DNS, with the routing CNAME record and the _acme-challenge CNAME record to add at your DNS provider" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for Valid

NitroCloud checks the record on its own, or you can click verify on the row. DNS changes can take up to 48 hours to be seen everywhere, though most are much quicker.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Domain status

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Pending DNS** | The CNAME is missing, or not visible yet. | Check the record at your DNS host, then wait or verify again. |
| **Verifying** | The record was found, and the certificate is being issued. | Nothing; it moves on by itself. |
| **Valid** | HTTPS is on, and the hostname serves this server. | Point your clients at the new hostname. |
| **Error** | The record doesn't match the target. | Fix the CNAME and verify again. |

The default `nitrocloud.ai` URL keeps working after the custom domain is valid, so clients that already use it don't break.

## Remove a domain

Remove a domain from its row. The hostname stops serving your server; the deployment itself isn't affected.
