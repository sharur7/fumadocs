An app is the unit you deploy in NitroCloud. Each app has two parts: an **MCP** module, which runs your MCP server, and a **Chat** module, a hosted chat that uses that server's tools. You manage both from the app's own pages.


## The apps list

Open **Apps** in the sidebar to see every app in your organization. The counters at the top show how many apps you have, how many have an MCP server linked, and how many have a chat linked. Each row shows whether the app's MCP and chat are live, or **Not deployed** yet.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/apps.png" alt="The Apps page: Total Apps, MCP linked, and Chat linked counters, above a list of apps with their slug, MCP and Chat links, date, and Live or Not deployed status" />

The counters also show how many MCP servers and chats are still **pending**, meaning they haven't been set up yet. In each row, the **MCP** and **Chat** labels are highlighted when that part of the app is linked, and the status on the right reads **Live** once the app is deployed. Use **Search apps** to find an app by name.

The card at the bottom of the sidebar shows how much of your organization's allowance you're using, such as **Apps 10 / 10** and **Chats 5 / 10**.

## Create an app

Creating an app makes an empty app; it doesn't deploy anything yet.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

Click **Create App** on the Apps page, or **Create New App** on the [dashboard](/docs/cloud/dashboard).

</Step>
<Step>

### Name the app

The **Create Nitrostack App** dialog asks for an **App name** and an optional **Description**. An app is one workspace for your MCP server, its chat, and future modules, and NitroCloud provisions the project behind it for you. The app's slug, made from its name, is used in the MCP URL and the chat URL. You can change the MCP slug later in [MCP settings](/docs/cloud/apps/mcp/settings), but doing so changes the public URL.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/create-app.png" alt="The Create Nitrostack App dialog with an App name field, an optional Description, and Cancel and Create App buttons" />

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the app

Click **Create App**, then open the new app. Its overview shows two modules, **MCP** and **Chat**, each **Not deployed** until you ship it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Deploy the server

Deploy the server from [MCP → Deployments](/docs/cloud/apps/mcp/deployments). The chat is created from **NitroChat** on the [MCP overview](/docs/cloud/apps/mcp), or opened with **Open Chat** once it exists.

</Step>
</Steps>

### Request more apps

Each organization can create up to 10 apps by default. When you reach the limit, creating an app opens **Request more Apps** instead. Under **Requested cap (apps)**, enter the app limit you need; the line below shows your current cap and how many apps you have. Explain why under **Why do you need this?**, for example that each tenant of a multi-tenant product needs its own MCP app, then click **Submit request**. The request goes to [Requests](/docs/cloud/team#requests), where an owner or admin approves a higher cap.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/request-apps.png" alt="The Request more Apps dialog, with the requested cap, the current cap and app count, a reason field, and a Submit request button" />

## The app overview

Click an app to open it. The sidebar switches to that app, with **All Apps** to go back.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/app-overview.png" alt="An app overview: the app name with a Live badge, the MCP and Chat modules with their status and Open buttons, and guides" />

The overview shows the app's description, slug, and creation date, then:

- **Modules** lists the MCP server and the chat, each with its status. **Open MCP** and **Open Chat** take you to their pages.
- **Guides** links to tutorials on the NitroStack blog.
- **Danger zone** has **Delete App**.

## Delete an app

**Delete App**, in the app's danger zone, removes the app with its server, its chat, its deployments, its domains, and its environment variables. You can't undo it. Owners and admins can delete an app; members can't.
