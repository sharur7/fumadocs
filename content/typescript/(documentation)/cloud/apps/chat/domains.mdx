Every chat gets a NitroCloud address ending in `.app.nitrocloud.ai`, shown on [General](/docs/cloud/apps/chat). **Domains**, in the Chat menu, lets you serve the same chat from a hostname you own, such as `chat.example.com`. It works the same way as [MCP domains](/docs/cloud/apps/mcp/domains), for the chat's hostname only.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/chat-domains.png" alt="The chat Domains page with a domain field showing chat.example.com, an Add button, and an empty state that says a CNAME record is required" />

## Add a domain

<Steps>
<Step>

### Enter the hostname

Type the hostname, such as `chat.example.com`, and click **Add**. Use a subdomain rather than your root domain.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the CNAME record

At your DNS host, create a `CNAME` record from that hostname to the target shown on the new row.

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for Valid

NitroCloud checks the record on its own, or you can click verify on the row. DNS changes can take up to 48 hours to be seen everywhere.

</Step>
</Steps>

The status on the row tells you where it stands: **Pending DNS** means the record isn't visible yet, **Verifying** means the certificate is being issued, **Valid** means the chat is live on the hostname, and **Error** means the record doesn't match the target. See [Domain status](/docs/cloud/apps/mcp/domains#domain-status) for what to do in each case.

## Update your embeds

The default chat URL keeps working after the custom domain is valid. Embed snippets that use the default URL don't switch over by themselves: if your website should load the chat from the new hostname, copy a fresh snippet from [General](/docs/cloud/apps/chat#embed-it-on-your-site).

## Remove a domain

Remove a domain from its row when you retire the hostname.
