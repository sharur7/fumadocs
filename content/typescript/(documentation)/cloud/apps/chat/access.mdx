**Access**, in the Chat menu, controls who can manage this chat. It lists the chat's members, the invitations that haven't been accepted yet, and a reference of what each role can do. Owners and admins can open it.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/chat-access.png" alt="The chat Access page, titled Team access: member, pending, and role counters, Members, Invitations, and Roles and Permissions tabs, and the member list" />

## Roles in a chat

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| **Owner** | Everything, including managing members and deleting the chat. |
| **Admin** | Change settings, deployments, and domains, and read logs, monitoring, and analytics. Can't delete the chat or manage members. |
| **Member** | View the chat, deploy, and read logs, monitoring, and analytics. |
| **Guest** | View the chat, logs, and monitoring. |

A person's role here applies to this chat only. It doesn't change their organization role on [Teams](/docs/cloud/team#roles).

## Invite someone

Click **Invite member**, enter their email address, and choose a role. The invitation gives them access to this chat through its linked MCP server. Invitations expire, and you can revoke a pending invitation before it's accepted.

## Change a role

When you change a member's role, the new role takes effect the next time they load the chat.
