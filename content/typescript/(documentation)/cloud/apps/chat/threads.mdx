**Threads** lists the conversations people have had with this chat. Open it from the Chat menu.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/chat-threads.png" alt="The Threads page: active conversations, total threads, and average latency at the top, the Timeline logs, Operational analytics, Errors, and Performance tabs, filters, and a table of threads" />

The counters at the top are **Active conversations** (sessions that are still open), **Total threads**, and **Average latency**. Treat average latency as informational for now: it isn't yet connected to the gateway, so don't rely on the number it shows.

## Timeline logs

The default view is **Timeline logs**. Each row is one thread: its ID, who spoke (the actor), its status, and when it was last updated. Open a row to read that conversation.

The filters work like this:

- **Actor ID** applies when you press Enter or click **Apply**.
- **Actor type**, **Status**, and **Model** apply as soon as you change them.
- **Has errors** limits the list to threads that recorded an error.
- **Reset** clears the filters, and **Refresh** reloads the list.

## Read one thread

Opening a thread shows that conversation: its messages, its tool calls, and any errors. It's the audit view for a single session, and the filters above are how you find the one you need.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/chat-thread-detail.png" alt="A thread open in a side panel: the user's message, the assistant's replies with model, time, and token counts, an MCP tool call with an expandable response, and conversation and usage details on the right" />

The conversation reads top to bottom. **User** messages are what the person typed. **Assistant** messages show the model that answered. **MCP tool** steps show each tool the assistant called; expand **Response** to see exactly what your server returned. The panel on the right shows who the actor is, when the thread started and was last updated, and its usage.

## Other views

If your role includes thread analytics, three more tabs appear:

- **Operational analytics** charts volume over time.
- **Errors** lists the threads and calls that failed.
- **Performance** splits results by LLM provider, by model, and by tool. Tool rows show how often each tool ran and whether it succeeded.

Guests don't get these tabs. They still see the timeline logs if they can open the chat.
