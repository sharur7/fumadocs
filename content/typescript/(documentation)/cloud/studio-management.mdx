**Studio Management** controls how your team uses AI in [NitroStudio](/docs/studio/overview): who has access, which models they can use, and how much they can spend.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/studio.png" alt="Studio settings: credit, member, and access policy summaries, a member list, and model configuration" />

## Summary

The cards at the top show your **Total credits**, as an available balance; **Enabled members**, the number of members with verified Studio access out of everyone in the organization; and your **Access policy**. The access policy shows how access is controlled, such as **RBAC enforced**, and the **Model pool**, such as **All Models Mode**.

## Member list

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/studio-members.png" alt="The Member list with Studio access, credit limit, additional usage, and usage columns, and the Model config card below it" />

**Member list** sets permissions and credit limits for each person:

- **Studio access** turns Studio on or off for the member.
- **Credit limit ($)** caps what the member can spend.
- **Additional usage ($)** allows spending beyond that limit.
- **Usage** shows what they've spent.

Click **Invite member** to add someone, and use **Find member** to search.

## Model config

**Model config** holds settings for the whole organization:

- **Enable all models** makes every model available. Top up credits to use it.
- **Usage limit** is the monthly spending ceiling for the whole organization, in dollars. It applies once the organization has left free token credits.

### Model pool

The **Model pool** in the summary card shows which models your team can call:

| Mode | What members can call |
|------|-----------------------|
| **Limited free models** | The default: a fixed set of free models. |
| **All paid models** | Paid models, after a credit top-up. |
| **All models** | Every model. Requires a top-up; **Enable all models** turns it on. |

The same list is what a hosted chat can choose from on its [AI model](/docs/cloud/apps/chat/ai-model) page.

## While you are on free credits

A new organization starts on free token credits, and a note at the top of the page says so. During this period, each member's **Credit limit** and **Additional usage** are saved but not enforced.

Your first credit top-up, on [Studio usage](/docs/cloud/usage#studio-usage), moves the organization to dollar billing. From then on, a member whose spending reaches their credit limit is blocked unless **Additional usage** allows more, and the organization's **Usage limit** still caps the whole team.

## Service status

All model requests go through a NitroStack proxy that sits in front of the model providers. **Status** and **Security status**, at the bottom of the page, show the state of that proxy. If the status isn't online, Studio's model calls fail even when a member has credits left.
