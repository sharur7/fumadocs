Two pages in the **Organization** part of the sidebar manage the people in your organization: **Team** and **Requests**.

## View your team

**Team** lists everyone in the organization, with their role and the date they joined. Use **Search members** to find someone, and **Refresh** to reload the list.

<Screenshot src="/images/cloud/team.png" alt="The Team page: a members table with each member's role and join date, Refresh and Invite member buttons, and an empty Pending invitations section" />

## Roles

Everyone in the organization has a role. The role controls what they can do on the organization's pages and, separately, inside each chat they can open.

| Role | In the organization | In a chat they can open |
|------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| **Owner** | Full access, including billing and deleting the organization. | Full access, including managing members and deleting the chat. |
| **Admin** | Manages settings, members, and apps, and the billing pages open to owners and admins. | Manages settings, deployments, and domains. Can't delete the chat. |
| **Member** | Uses the apps they're allowed to open. | Views the chat, deploys, and reads logs, monitoring, and analytics. Can't change settings. |
| **Guest** | Read-only, wherever they're invited. | Views the chat, logs, and monitoring. |

A person's role in a particular chat can be changed on that chat's [Access](/docs/cloud/apps/chat/access) page without changing their organization role.

## Invite a member

Click **Invite member** and enter their email address. NitroCloud sends them an invitation email, and the invitation waits under **Pending invitations** until they accept it. They accept it from **Invitations** in the top bar.

You can also invite people from [Studio management](/docs/cloud/studio-management). Studio access is a separate switch there: an invitation doesn't turn on Studio for the person by itself.

## Requests

**Requests**, in the sidebar, is the queue for things members can't do themselves, such as raising the organization's [app limit](/docs/cloud/apps#request-more-apps). Approve or reject each request. When nothing is waiting, the page shows **All caught up**.
